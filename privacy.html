Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers the Production Ready channel and the private publishing tool ("the app") the channel owner uses to post their own videos to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. The app is not offered to the public and has a single user: the channel owner.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok: the basic profile of the channel's own TikTok account (open ID, display name, avatar) and the videos the app posts to that account, including their upload status.
  • YouTube: through the YouTube Data and Analytics APIs, the channel's own videos, metadata, playlists, comments and analytics, used to upload, schedule and manage the channel's videos.
  • Instagram: through the Instagram Graph API, the channel's own account ID and the Reels it publishes.

Content we post

The app only posts content created by the channel owner, to the channel owner's own accounts. It never posts on behalf of anyone else.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish and manage the channel's own videos and to show the owner their own results. Access tokens and data are stored privately on the owner's own server and are never published.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. We do not use it for advertising.

Viewers

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers and collects no personal data from visitors.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok, Google or Meta account settings, after which the app can no longer access the account. Stored data is deleted on request.

Contact

Use the contact form, or message the channel on YouTube or Instagram.
